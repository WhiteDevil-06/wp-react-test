# HYLOS DESIGN SYSTEM — ORBITAL EDITORIAL

Authoritative visual design specification for the official Hylos digital brand presence.

---

## 1. CORE AESTHETIC

**Design Name**: Orbital Editorial

**Core Aesthetic**: Premium B2B editorial design + modern corporate sophistication + human influence networks.

The visual language combines:
- **Editorial Authority**: High contrast typography, generous line heights, asymmetric structured layouts, refined whitespace.
- **Human Connection**: Focus on people, ideas, founder authority, and strategic trust.
- **Controlled Precision**: Architectural grids, sharp architectural radii, precise lines.
- **Orbital Geometry**: Fine-line network nodes, influence arcs, expanding radii representing authority radiating into demand.

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

- **Primary Editorial Font**: `Hanken Grotesk` (Headings & Display)
- **Body Font**: `Inter` (Body & Narrative Text)
- **Technical/Metadata Font**: `JetBrains Mono` (Labels, Badges, Metrics, Metadata)

### Hierarchy Specifications:
1. **Display XL**:
   - Font: `Hanken Grotesk` | Weight: 800 | Size: 80px | Line Height: 88px | Letter Spacing: -0.04em
   - Mobile: 48px | Weight: 800 | Line Height: 52px | Letter Spacing: -0.02em
2. **Headline Large**:
   - Font: `Hanken Grotesk` | Weight: 700 | Size: 40px | Line Height: 48px
   - Mobile: 32px | Weight: 700 | Line Height: 38px
3. **Body Large**:
   - Font: `Inter` | Weight: 400 | Size: 18px | Line Height: 32px
4. **Body Medium**:
   - Font: `Inter` | Weight: 400 | Size: 16px | Line Height: 24px
5. **Technical Labels**:
   - Font: `JetBrains Mono` | Weight: 500 | Size: 12px | Line Height: 16px | Letter Spacing: 0.1em (uppercase)

---

## 4. LAYOUT & GRID SYSTEM

- **Desktop Grid**: 12 columns | Max container: 1440px | Gutter: 32px | Margin: 64px
- **Mobile Grid**: 4 fluid columns | Margin: 24px
- **Major Section Gap**: 120px
- **Layout Principle**: Asymmetric editorial layout, deliberate whitespace, non-centered editorial grids.

---

## 5. SHAPE & ELEVATION

- **Shape Language**: Subtle, architectural corner radius (default: 4px). Avoid rounded pill cards.
- **Elevation**: Tonal layering over shadows. 1px precise structural borders (`#bdc8d0` or `#3d484f`). No indiscriminate glassmorphism.

---

## 6. BUTTON SYSTEM

- **Primary Action**: Hylos Cyan (`#06B3ED`) fill, solid text, zero gradient.
- **Secondary Action**: Ghost / Editorial button with 1px border & technical label styling.
- **Conversion-Critical Action**: Hylos Orange (`#EA6523`) fill, used intentionally for primary lead capture CTAs.

---

## 7. ORBITAL GEOMETRY

- Fine line arcs, influence radii, network nodes, and radiating circles.
- Symbolizes: **People → Ideas → Content → Audience → Authority → Demand**.
