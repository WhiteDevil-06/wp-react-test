# Visual Language Guidelines

- **Logo Protection**: Official logo asset only. Never recreate, redraft, or apply generic AI graphics.
- **Color Discipline**: Deep editorial background (`#f5faff` light / `#0B0F14` dark reference), Cyan (`#06B3ED`) for digital network structure, Orange (`#EA6523`) exclusively for human influence and intent actions.
- **Imagery**: Real human influence, high-end executive typography, restrained network geometry. Avoid AI brain clichés, cybersecurity shields, or circuit boards.
