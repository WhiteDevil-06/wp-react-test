# On-Page SEO Specifications

## Page Title Format
`[Page Title] | Hylos — B2B Marketing & Founder Authority`

## Metadata Limits
- **Meta Title**: Max 60 characters.
- **Meta Description**: Max 155 characters, concise action-oriented copy.

## Heading Hierarchy Rules
- Exactly **one** `<h1>` per page containing the target primary keyword.
- Structured `<h2>` and `<h3>` tags with clear topic hierarchy.
- No skipped heading levels (e.g. `<h1>` followed directly by `<h3>`).
- Alt attributes required on all image elements.
