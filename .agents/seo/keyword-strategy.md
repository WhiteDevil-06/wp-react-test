# Hylos Keyword Strategy

## Core Pillars & Primary Intent

1. **LinkedIn Executive & Founder Branding**:
   - Target Terms: B2B founder branding, LinkedIn personal branding for tech leaders, executive positioning, LinkedIn company growth.
   - Search Intent: High-intent B2B decision makers seeking personal authority and lead generation via LinkedIn.

2. **AI Search & Intent-Based SEO**:
   - Target Terms: AI SEO agency, generative search optimization, intent-driven SEO, B2B organic growth strategy.
   - Search Intent: Tech firms wanting visibility in traditional search and AI engine queries (ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity).

3. **B2B Performance & Content Marketing**:
   - Target Terms: B2B demand generation agency, tech content marketing, B2B growth marketing partner.
   - Search Intent: B2B tech companies seeking revenue pipeline growth through content-led marketing.
