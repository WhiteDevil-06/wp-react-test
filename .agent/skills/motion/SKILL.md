# Motion Skill

## Guidelines
1. Keep animations purposeful, smooth, and performance-first (`duration: 0.5s` to `0.8s`, `ease: [0.16, 1, 0.3, 1]`).
2. Entrance animations for Hero text, dynamic card grids, and section headers.
3. Micro-interactions: subtle glow escalation and translate Y on card hover.
4. Avoid layout shift or heavy repaints.
